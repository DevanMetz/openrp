OPENRP.
PUBLIC ALPHA · DESKTOP BROWSER

A city worth playing in.

OpenRP is a free, open-source multiplayer roleplay sandbox inspired by DarkRP. It is an independent project, unaffiliated with Garry’s Mod or Facepunch. Bring a keyboard, a mouse, and a story.

Community rules

  1. Give conflict a roleplay reason. No random killing, spawn camping, or repeatedly targeting a player to ruin their session.
  2. Build fairly. Don’t block public roads, spawns, or other players’ exits. No trapping people with props or using props to bypass fights.
  3. Respect the people behind the characters. No harassment, hate speech, threats, doxxing, or sexual content. Keep personal information out of chat and character names.
  4. Play without exploits. No cheats, connection spam, impersonating operators, or creating identities to manipulate money or elections.

Operators can remove or ban identities that break these rules. City laws chosen by the mayor are part of roleplay; they cannot override community rules.

What to expect in the alpha

Jobs, shops, property, physics building, printers, elections, combat and arrests work in a shared city. There is no fixed player slot cap. Performance depends on server load. Vehicles, voice chat, and Source-engine assets are not included.

Your wallet returns in the same browser. Jobs, inventory, props, and property reset when you leave or the server restarts. Updates can briefly interrupt play. Clearing browser data loses your anonymous identity; there is no account recovery yet.

Privacy

No email, password, or payment is needed. Your browser stores a randomly generated reconnect credential, name and settings. The server stores your name, wallet, an internal ID and a hash of that credential. Bans store an identity ID and operator reason. Chat is sent to players in its channel and is not archived by the game.

Hosting and DNS providers (Railway and Cloudflare) process network requests and may keep operational logs including IP addresses. The game uses source IPs temporarily to limit connection spam. There are no advertising trackers or third-party game assets.

For a bug report or data request, use the project’s issue tracker. Share your public roleplay name and approximate time, never your browser credential or sensitive personal information. Use GitHub’s private vulnerability reporting for security issues when available.
